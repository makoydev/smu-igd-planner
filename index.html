After the taught modules

The IGD is not finished at module 12

Graduation also requires the overall IGD exam and one of the two applied/enrichment routes, all within the official candidature period.

Route A: Integrated Learning Project (ILP)

10 weeksteam-basedportfolio output
  • Best for a demonstrable GenAI/RAG/governance project.
  • Teams of up to five with faculty guidance.
  • Minimum class size applies; future dates must be confirmed.

Route B: Enrichment workshops / networking

16 sessions4 hours eachflexible
  • Best for scheduling flexibility and broader career skills.
  • Track attendance carefully across all 16 sessions.
  • Schedules can change and minimum class size applies.
Exam strategy: attempt the first eligible exam after all module results are posted. SMU states there is no supplementary exam; after a failure, the learner must register for the next scheduled exam.
